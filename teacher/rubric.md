  <span class="section-number">Teacher Resource</span>
  <h1>Lesson Rubric</h1>
</div>

<p>Single rubric covering all deliverables from Sections 1–7. Section 8 (ABS Trap) has a separate advanced rubric below. Total: 100 points (core) + 25 points (advanced).</p>

<h2>Core Lesson — 100 Points</h2>

<h3>Section 1: Physics Foundations (15 pts)</h3>

<div class="table-wrap">
<table>
  <thead>
    <tr><th>Criteria</th><th>Excellent (15)</th><th>Proficient (12)</th><th>Developing (8)</th><th>Incomplete (0–4)</th></tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>Knowledge Check — 3 calculations</td>
      <td>All 3 correct with work shown and correct units</td>
      <td>2 of 3 correct, or all correct but missing units</td>
      <td>1 of 3 correct, or calculations attempted with significant errors</td>
      <td>Not attempted or no correct answers</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h3>Section 2: FEA Workflow (10 pts)</h3>

<div class="table-wrap">
<table>
  <thead>
    <tr><th>Criteria</th><th>Excellent (10)</th><th>Proficient (8)</th><th>Developing (5)</th><th>Incomplete (0–2)</th></tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>Workflow labeling — 7 steps identified as human or computer</td>
      <td>All 7 correctly labeled, including "semi-automatic" for mesh</td>
      <td>5–6 correct, or all correct but mesh not noted as semi-automatic</td>
      <td>3–4 correct</td>
      <td>Not attempted or fewer than 3 correct</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h3>Section 3: Simulation Setup (20 pts)</h3>

<div class="table-wrap">
<table>
  <thead>
    <tr><th>Criteria</th><th>Excellent (5 ea.)</th><th>Proficient (4 ea.)</th><th>Developing (2 ea.)</th><th>Incomplete (0)</th></tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>Material assignment screenshot</td>
      <td>Custom FDM PLA with all 5 properties correct and visible</td>
      <td>Custom material created but 1 property incorrect or unclear</td>
      <td>Used built-in material or multiple properties wrong</td>
      <td>Missing</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Fixture placement screenshot</td>
      <td>Correctly placed on both support locations, arrows visible</td>
      <td>Correct locations but screenshot unclear</td>
      <td>Incorrect placement (wrong face, over-constrained)</td>
      <td>Missing</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Load application screenshot</td>
      <td>Correct face, correct direction, correct magnitude, arrow visible</td>
      <td>Correct setup but magnitude or screenshot unclear</td>
      <td>Wrong face, wrong direction, or wrong magnitude</td>
      <td>Missing</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Results summary</td>
      <td>Max stress, max displacement, min FoS all recorded with values in reasonable range</td>
      <td>All three recorded but one value seems off</td>
      <td>Missing one or more values</td>
      <td>Missing</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h3>Section 4: Results Interpretation (10 pts)</h3>

<div class="table-wrap">
<table>
  <thead>
    <tr><th>Criteria</th><th>Excellent (10)</th><th>Proficient (8)</th><th>Developing (5)</th><th>Incomplete (0–2)</th></tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>Interpret provided results screenshot: identify max stress location, displacement, min FoS, and write 2-sentence summary</td>
      <td>All 4 elements correct. Summary demonstrates understanding of what FoS means practically.</td>
      <td>3 of 4 elements correct. Summary present but imprecise.</td>
      <td>1–2 elements correct. Summary vague or missing.</td>
      <td>Not attempted</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h3>Section 5: Print Documentation (10 pts)</h3>

<div class="table-wrap">
<table>
  <thead>
    <tr><th>Criteria</th><th>Excellent (10)</th><th>Proficient (8)</th><th>Developing (5)</th><th>Incomplete (0–2)</th></tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>Bambu Studio settings screenshot + labeled specimen photos</td>
      <td>Screenshot shows all required settings (100% infill, 0.2mm, correct temps). 3+ labeled specimens with correct naming convention.</td>
      <td>Settings correct but screenshot missing one parameter. Or 3+ specimens but naming convention incomplete.</td>
      <td>Settings partially correct. Fewer than 3 specimens. Or no labeling.</td>
      <td>Missing documentation</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h3>Section 6: Physical Testing (15 pts)</h3>

<div class="table-wrap">
<table>
  <thead>
    <tr><th>Criteria</th><th>Excellent (5 ea.)</th><th>Proficient (4 ea.)</th><th>Developing (2 ea.)</th><th>Incomplete (0)</th></tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>Data sheet completeness</td>
      <td>All load steps recorded with mass, force, deflection. Failure clearly noted.</td>
      <td>Most steps recorded, minor gaps</td>
      <td>Significant gaps or only failure recorded</td>
      <td>Missing</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Calculations</td>
      <td>Flexural stress and modulus correctly calculated with work shown</td>
      <td>One calculation correct, one with minor error</td>
      <td>Formulas identified but significant math errors</td>
      <td>Missing</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Specimen photo</td>
      <td>Clear photo showing failure location with label visible</td>
      <td>Photo present but failure location unclear</td>
      <td>Photo present but poor quality, can't see failure</td>
      <td>Missing</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h3>Section 7: Comparison + Reflection (20 pts)</h3>

<div class="table-wrap">
<table>
  <thead>
    <tr><th>Criteria</th><th>Excellent (10 ea.)</th><th>Proficient (8 ea.)</th><th>Developing (5 ea.)</th><th>Incomplete (0–2)</th></tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>Comparison table</td>
      <td>All fields complete. Percent error correctly calculated. Failure location comparison includes Y/N match assessment.</td>
      <td>Table complete but percent error has minor calculation error</td>
      <td>Table partially complete or significant errors</td>
      <td>Missing</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Written reflection (3–5 sentences)</td>
      <td>States percent error. Names 2+ specific, plausible error sources. Proposes specific improvements for closer match.</td>
      <td>States percent error. Names 1 error source. Proposes a general improvement.</td>
      <td>Vague reflection without specific analysis</td>
      <td>Missing or single sentence</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2>Section 8: The ABS Trap — Advanced (25 bonus pts)</h2>

<div class="table-wrap">
<table>
  <thead>
    <tr><th>Criteria</th><th>Excellent (pts shown)</th><th>Proficient</th><th>Developing</th><th>Incomplete</th></tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>Side-by-side comparison table (10 pts)</td>
      <td>All three columns complete (default ABS, corrected ABS, physical). Percent errors calculated for both.</td>
      <td>Table complete but one percent error incorrect (8)</td>
      <td>Table partially complete (5)</td>
      <td>Missing (0)</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Written reflection (15 pts)</td>
      <td>Explains why default ABS produced larger error. Connects to injection-molded vs. FDM difference. Articulates the general principle about verifying simulation inputs. (15)</td>
      <td>Identifies the material mismatch but doesn't generalize the lesson (11)</td>
      <td>Vague explanation without connecting to material assumptions (7)</td>
      <td>Missing (0)</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2>Grade Scale</h2>

<div class="table-wrap">
<table>
  <thead>
    <tr><th>Score</th><th>Grade</th><th>Description</th></tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>90–100</td><td>A</td><td>Thorough understanding. Accurate calculations. Insightful reflection.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>80–89</td><td>B</td><td>Solid understanding. Minor errors in calculations or reflection.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>70–79</td><td>C</td><td>Basic understanding. Some gaps in documentation or analysis.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>60–69</td><td>D</td><td>Partial completion. Significant gaps.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>&lt; 60</td><td>F</td><td>Incomplete. Major sections missing.</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
</div>

<div class="callout callout-info">
  <span class="callout-title">Advanced Credit</span>
  <p>Section 8 points (up to 25) are added on top of the 100-point core. A student who earns 92/100 on the core + 22/25 on Section 8 = 114/100. Handle as extra credit per your grading policy.</p>
